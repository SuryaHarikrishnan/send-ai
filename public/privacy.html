Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

SendIt is a climbing log and AI training coach. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We do not get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Your climbing data. The climbs, sessions and notes you log in the app.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your climbing log tied to your account.
  • To show your progress and analytics.
  • To generate coaching tips. When you use the AI coach, a summary of your recent climbs is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce a response.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your account and climbing data are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. The app is hosted on Vercel.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual climbs in the app at any time. To delete your whole account and all its data, contact us and we will remove it.
